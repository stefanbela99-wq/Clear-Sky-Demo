My Approach

Clear advice, built around you

I started Clear Sky Consulting because I believe good business advice shouldn't be complicated, expensive or full of jargon. Whether you run a small business or you're a private client with an idea worth pursuing, my job is to help you see the path ahead clearly and take confident steps forward.

I work entirely online, which means we can meet in a way that fits your life — early mornings, evenings, or between school runs. Every engagement is personal: I take the time to understand your situation before offering practical, honest recommendations you can act on straight away.

  • Small business owners looking to grow or stabilise
  • Private clients planning a new venture or side project
  • Anyone who wants a clear, unbiased second opinion
