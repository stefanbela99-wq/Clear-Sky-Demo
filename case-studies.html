Decision clarity

Two offers, one life - choosing without the fear

Subiaco, WA

The situation

Rebecca, an operations manager in her late thirties, had two offers on the table: a senior role interstate with a substantial pay rise, and a steadier promotion that kept her family close. Six weeks of pro-and-con lists had only deepened the fog.

What we did

In a single 90-minute session we set the spreadsheets aside and mapped what she was really choosing between - not two jobs, but two versions of the next five years. We pressure-tested each against what mattered most to her, and named the one regret she knew she couldn't live with.

The outcome

Rebecca turned down the bigger salary and took the local promotion, leaving the session certain rather than anxious. Three months on, she told us it was the clearest decision she'd made in a decade.
